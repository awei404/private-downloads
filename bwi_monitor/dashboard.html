Alert history — last 24 h (resolved, late, muted)

Internal alert rules BWI-V1.7 — not official V2 KPI results. Unload countdown starts at MAX(actual arrival, warehouse opening) — never at Start Shift, which only turns alerts on; reminders at 30 and 55 min, overdue at 60 min; single-stop: all cargo unloaded, multi-stop: first unloading scan. Times are judged from 10-minute TMS polls ("based on TMS records"). Operation compliance is separate: MISSING = missing in TMS records, RISK = not recorded yet, UNCERTAIN = TMS data cannot tell. Acknowledging an alert does not complete the TMS operation.
